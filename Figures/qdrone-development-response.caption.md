QDrone2 tracked 20 complete altitude steps during one recorded discharge. Development observations under the original MPC; no recovery-failure labels. 20 complete response windows; 1 incomplete (grey dotted line). (a) Observed altitude [m] (blue) follows the recorded reference steps (grey dashed). (b) Battery voltage [V] declines across the recording and swings at each command. (c) and (d) 2 s altitude RMSE [m] of each complete step against command time [s] and precommand voltage [V]; up steps blue triangles, down steps orange inverted triangles. Every down step has a higher RMSE than every up step, and the gap holds across the precommand voltage range. Repeated commands share one recording. Demand, voltage and elapsed time co-vary.
